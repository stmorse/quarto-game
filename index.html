How to play

Sixteen pieces, each one tall or short, light or dark, round or square, solid or hollow. No two are alike.

The twist: you never choose your own piece. You pick a piece and hand it to your opponent, they place it anywhere on the board, and then they pick the piece for you.

You win by placing the fourth piece of a line — row, column or diagonal — in which all four pieces share at least one attribute. Turn on the 2×2 variant and any square block counts too. Sixteen pieces down with no line is a draw.

The board calls “Quarto!” for you: a completed line is detected and highlighted automatically.
